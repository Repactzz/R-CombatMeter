R-CombatMeter | v1.0.10
World of Warcraft: Forever

LANGUAGE
The addon uses German on German game clients and English otherwise.
Language is selected automatically when the addon loads.

CONTROLS
- Choose damage or healing (current or overall), interrupts, dispels or deaths in the color-coded header.
- Deaths is only available if the client supports that meter type.
- Use + to add a standalone window in the center or insert an attached window directly above the selected window. Drag the header to move and dock windows.
- Positions and lock states persist across /reload.
- Lock the group with the padlock. Drag the lower-right corner to resize the group.
- Click the scroll icon to select past fights retained by the game, or return to live data.
- Enemy portraits are cached from observed units; unavailable portraits use a neutral icon.
- Click a player row to open an attached ability panel above that row, matching the meter size; click again to close. At the screen edge it may shrink or open below.
- Open the guide to change accent and header colors, layout, or demo preview.
- Settings > Profiles: select, reset or delete account-wide character profiles.
- Existing per-character settings migrate on first login. Changes are saved to the active profile.

COMMANDS
/rcm        Open guide / settings
/rcm demo   Toggle animated demo preview
/rcm hide   Toggle all windows
/rcm lock   Toggle group locks
/rcm reset  Reset shared combat data after confirmation

Auto-reset starts only Damage Done Overall when entering or leaving an instance.
The history and other modes remain unchanged. Protected totals update when readable.
The manual reset command still clears shared Blizzard data after confirmation.
