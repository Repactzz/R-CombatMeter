R-CombatMeter | v1.0.5
World of Warcraft: Forever

LANGUAGE
The addon uses German on German game clients and English otherwise.
Language is selected automatically when the addon loads.

CONTROLS
- Choose damage or healing, current fight or overall, in the header.
- Use + to add a standalone window in the center or insert an attached window directly above the selected window. Drag the header to move and dock windows.
- Positions and lock states persist across /reload.
- Lock the group with the padlock. Drag the lower-right corner to resize the group.
- Click the scroll icon to select past fights retained by the game, or return to live data.
- Open the guide to change accent and header colors, layout, or demo preview.

COMMANDS
/rcm        Open guide / settings
/rcm demo   Toggle animated demo preview
/rcm hide   Toggle all windows
/rcm lock   Toggle group locks
/rcm reset  Reset shared combat data after confirmation

Auto-reset is configurable in the guide. It resets shared Blizzard combat data,
which can also affect other meters. Historical fights are available until reset.
